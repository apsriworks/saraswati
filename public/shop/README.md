# Shop Photos Directory

Upload your store photos here so they appear automatically on your website!

Recommended image filenames:
- `frontage.jpg` : Front entrance & signboard of Saraswathi Super Market
- `aisles.jpg` : Grocery aisles view inside the store
- `vegetables.jpg` : Fresh produce and vegetable display area
- `counter.jpg` : Billing counter or storefront overview

Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`

The website will automatically load these store photos with fallback images until you upload your custom photos!
